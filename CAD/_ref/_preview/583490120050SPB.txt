Type 58. 34 is a relay interface module, 4 CO 7 A, with screw terminals, 27 mm wide.  
For interfacing with PLC systems. Available Atex compliant version (HazLoc Class I  
Div. 2 Groups A, B, C, D – T5 compliant): 58. 34-x0xx, 4 CO 6 A, with scren terminals  
or push-in terminals. Features include: AC or DC coil, Coil Indication and EMC  
suppression module, Identification label, UL Listing (relay/socket/jumper link), 35 mm  
rail (EN 60715) mounting, Cadmium-free contacts
